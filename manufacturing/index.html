Top Story

Ransomware halts smart-meter production and shipments at three Osaki Electric plants after a VPN login without MFA

Osaki Electric, Japan’s largest smart-meter maker, confirmed on 17 November that ransomware had encrypted some of its virtual servers. It cut external connections and on 18 November shut down production-related systems at its Saitama Works and at group companies Iwate Osaki Electric and Osaki Platec, suspending production and shipments. The company says the production equipment itself was not encrypted; systems were stopped to keep the infection from spreading. Its 20 November update says the intruder most likely got in on 15 November through a VPN device, by repeated password attempts on an account without multi-factor authentication, some 57 hours before encryption. Technical information believed to be its own has been posted online, and Qilin has listed the company on its leak site. No restart date has been given. Precautionary shutdowns like this are how ransomware usually stops a factory (editorial assessment).
