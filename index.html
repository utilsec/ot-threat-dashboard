FERC has approved NERC’s Reliability Standard CIP-014-4, the physical security standard for transmission stations and substations whose loss could cause instability, uncontrolled separation or cascading across an interconnection. The Federal Register notice published on 15 September sets an effective date of 1 October 2028, with CIP-014-3 retiring immediately beforehand. The substance is mostly about rigour. Requirement R3 now obliges transmission owners to maintain a documented risk-assessment methodology with technically justified thresholds for instability, uncontrolled separation and cascading, both steady-state and dynamic simulations run against minimum peak and off-peak load cases, and stated assumptions for loss of communications, loss of protection systems, faults and clearing times. Requirement R2 adds a proximity test: identify Bulk Electric System stations and substations within 1,500 feet (457 m), fence line to fence line, regardless of who owns them. Requirement R5 collapses the old two-tier 30- and 60-month reassessment cycle into a single 36-month cycle aligned with TPL-001 planning assessments, and Requirement R4 forces joint owners to write down who does what. FERC estimates 344 transmission owners are affected.

Regulatory action, not a threat — confirmed: FERC approved CIP-014-4 and the associated implementation plan, violation risk factors and violation severity levels, and approved retirement of CIP-014-3, per the Federal Register notice published 15 Sep 2026. NERC filed the standard on 16 Jul 2026 under Project 2023-06, itself a response to FERC’s Dec 2022 directive following the Moore County, North Carolina and Pacific Northwest substation attacks. NERC had concluded that the existing applicability criteria were adequate but that R1 risk assessments lacked technical rigour and consistency, and rejected a uniform minimum-protection baseline in favour of risk-tailored controls paired with resiliency measures such as response readiness and spare-equipment strategies. Requirements R6 through R10 carry forward the former CIP-014-3 R2 through R6 without material change.
