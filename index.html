Between 26–27 July 2026 a coordinated campaign disrupted automated controls at more than 30 Minnesota community water systems, and confirmed intrusions have since been reported across roughly a dozen states including Georgia, Michigan, South Dakota, Alabama and New Jersey. Three airports lost runway capacity to drones in a week — and one of the drones was carrying explosives

On the night of 5–6 August 2026 an airport worker at Leipzig/Halle found a drone carrying an explosive device near the south runway; the detonator had separated, preventing detonation, and an EOD robot removed it. These are physical and hybrid incidents rather than cyber compromises of airport systems — but they hit the same target that a cyber attack on airfield systems would, and the Hannover detection failure is a control gap, not a policy one.

NTSB is investigating a fatal medevac crash that followed GPS loss during a military jamming exercise

The NTSB is investigating the crash of a Beechcraft King Air C90 medevac aircraft into New Mexico’s Capitan Mountains on 14 May 2026, which killed two pilots and two nurses. The aircraft lost GPS signal during an active military GPS-jamming exercise at White Sands; three other aircraft reported GPS loss the same night. Be precise about what is established: the NTSB confirms the jamming exercise took place and that GPS was lost, but has not determined probable cause or contributing factors, so any causal link remains unproven. A CVSS 10.0 command injection in Haiwell’s cloud HMI gateway hands an unauthenticated attacker root

CISA advisory ICSA-26-225-02 covers CVE-2026-19188, an OS command injection (CWE-78) in the Haiwell IoT Cloud HMI Gateway that scores the full CVSS 10.0.
