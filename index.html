FERC approved a rewritten substation physical-security standard — and the new risk assessment now has to look over the fence at the substation next door

FERC approved NERC Reliability Standard CIP-014-4 on 10 September, published in the Federal Register on 15 September. The rewrite does not widen which substations are in scope; it tightens how transmission owners prove the ones they picked are the right ones. The applicable-facility list must be reviewed at least every 36 months and must include stations planned to be in service within that window. A new Requirement R2 makes owners identify proximate bulk electric system stations and substations within 1,500 feet, fence line to fence line, irrespective of ownership, and feed them into the risk assessment. The single 36-month assessment cycle replaces today’s split 30/60-month cadence, which shortens the maximum re-look for owners who previously found nothing critical from five years to three.
