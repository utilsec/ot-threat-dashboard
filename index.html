A frontier model ported a working PLC exploit to a second controller in eight hours — then bricked it

Exploitation and CISA KEV tags reflect status at generation time — “No confirmed exploitation reported / Not in CISA KEV” means none was reported as of this edition, not that risk is absent. Suggested SLAs are starting points (Critical ≈ 7 days, High ≈ 30 days) to adapt to your own risk tolerance. Owner and status notes you enter are saved only in your local browser and are not transmitted anywhere.

Methodology. Compiled on 2 September 2026 by searching authoritative OT/ICS sources including CISA ICS advisories and the 25, 27 August and 1 September batches, the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue additions of 24–31 August, CISA’s internet exposure-reduction guidance, the NSA/CISA/FBI/DOE/EPA joint advisory AA26-231A on Siemens S7 series programmable logic controllers, the FBI/EPA public service announcement of 30 July on internet-facing programmable logic controllers in the water sector, the SonicWall PSIRT advisory SNWLID-2026-0016 on exploited SMA1000 zero-days, the PaperCut security bulletin of 27 August with WatchTowr, Huntress and Rapid7 exploitation analyses, Forescout Research – Vedere Labs’ AI-assisted PLC exploit-porting report, the Congressional Research Service assessment of federal water-sector cyber protections, the White House and Texas Cyber Command announcement of Project Watershed 250, the US Coast Guard Maritime Commons announcement establishing the Office of Maritime Cybersecurity Policy, Google Cloud’s water-sector hardening guidance, Sygnia’s research on the Fire Ant intrusion cluster, the Rockwell Automation security advisory index behind the 1 September ICS batch, Reuters’ reporting carrying company and FBI confirmation of the Micro-Comm water-sector supplier breach, the White House executive order declaring a national emergency over foreign-produced bulk-power system equipment, the ISA and OT Cybersecurity Coalition joint announcement of their memorandum of understanding on ISA/IEC 62443 harmonisation, the UK NCSC statement on disruptive activity against internet-exposed systems and edge devices, Claroty Team82’s cyber-physical attack-trend analysis, and Accenture’s newsroom material on its pending OT security acquisitions — corroborated against reporting from SecurityWeek, Industrial Cyber, The Record, CyberScoop, Cybersecurity Dive, BleepingComputer, The Hacker News, StateScoop, Nextgov/FCW and Federal News Network. Every advisory and vulnerability claim was checked against a primary source before publication, and items whose source date could not be confirmed within 30 days were dropped.
