High Advisory — OpenSSH “SSHole” authentication bypass CVE-2026-123158 (CVSS 8.1) on musl-based Linux without PAM; exploited within hours; added to CISA KEV (9 Nov 2026)

OpenSSH “SSHole” lets an over-long password open password-less accounts on musl-based Linux; exploited within hours and now in KEV

OpenSSH 10.6p2 fixes CVE-2026-123158 (CVSS 8.1). On Linux builds that use the musl C library with PAM turned off, a password of 257 to 1,024 characters is accepted for accounts that have no usable password, including root on default Alpine setups. Previdian saw the first attempt less than nine hours after disclosure on 6 November and confirmed root on an Alpine honeypot; by 8 November it counted 20 attempts from 13 addresses, and watchTowr says only botnets and crypto miners have used it so far. CISA has added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Glibc-based distributions, Dropbear and systems that check passwords through PAM are not affected. musl-based embedded builds such as Buildroot, OpenWrt and Yocto sit inside many industrial gateways, routers and appliances, but no OT vendor has yet published an advisory, so whether a given product is exposed depends on how it was built (editorial assessment).

Exploited in the wild — Confirmed vs claimed: exploitation is confirmed by Previdian honeypot data, GreyNoise, Defused and CISA’s KEV listing; observed use is botnets and miners; no OT product or victim has been confirmed In CISA KEV (added 9 Nov 2026); CVE-2026-123158
Upgrade to OpenSSH 10.6p2 or the distribution’s patched package and restart sshd; or disable password authentication Action: Ask suppliers of Linux-based gateways, routers, edge servers and appliances whether their images use musl with OpenSSH and no PAM, and get their patch dates in writing. On anything you manage yourself, disable SSH password authentication or update to 10.6p2, keep SSH off the internet and off routable user networks, and check logs for password logins to root or key-only accounts. If one cannot be ruled out, treat the device as compromised.
