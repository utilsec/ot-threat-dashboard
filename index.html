Citrix NetScaler zero-days exploited for weeks before disclosure, CISA warns

Two Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway zero-days, CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772, are under global exploitation, and researchers say intruders were inside appliances for weeks before Citrix disclosed on 27 September.
