Cyberattack on Brazil’s Santos port authority forces manual workarounds at Latin America’s busiest port

The Port of Santos authority, which runs Brazil’s largest port, lost its IT systems on 6 November and on 10 November confirmed a cyberattack. Medium Advisory — Elecbrakes EB2, VM1 and ELBC 2000 Bluetooth trailer brake controllers (CISA ICSA-26-323-02); two critical flaws; no exploitation reported; cross-sector, carried for agriculture as editorial assessment

Bluetooth flaws let a nearby attacker apply or release trailer brakes on Elecbrakes controllers, and the oldest model is still unpatched

Element Digital researchers found three Bluetooth flaws in Elecbrakes trailer brake controllers (EB2, VM1 and ELBC 2000), two rated critical, covering missing authentication and weak replay protection. An attacker within about 100 metres could engage or release a trailer’s brakes, or change braking force, without the driver doing anything. A third, medium-severity firmware-update flaw is considered theoretical. CISA published advisory ICSA-26-323-02 last week. Elecbrakes shipped firmware for EB2 and VM1 in early July and says about 60–70% of controllers in daily use have it; a fix for the older ELBC 2000 is still in progress. There is no evidence of malicious exploitation. For farms and haulers towing livestock, grain or equipment trailers, this is a road-safety issue on a wireless link, not a plant-network one (editorial assessment).

Medium Policy — GAO-27-108656, Critical Infrastructure Protection: Further Action Needed to Enhance Cybersecurity of the Food and Agriculture Sector (published 19 Nov 2026); 5 recommendations to USDA and HHS/FDA

GAO finds USDA and FDA fall short on cyber threat sharing and emergency exercises for the food and agriculture sector

GAO reported on 19 November that USDA and the FDA, the sector’s lead federal agencies, met most cyber-related requirements Congress set in the FY2021 National Defense Authorization Act but only partially met or missed five, mainly on threat-information sharing and emergency preparedness. Both agencies cited a lack of resources and staffing, and ABC News reports they have not run food-sector cyberattack response exercises since 2024. GAO made five recommendations: two to USDA’s Office of Homeland Security and three to the FDA’s Office of Coordinated Outbreak Response, covering information sharing with CISA, preparedness exercises and, for FDA, planning documents for cyber emergencies. HHS agreed; USDA disagreed with its two and asked GAO to remove or redirect them, and GAO kept them. GAO recalls the 2021 ransomware attack on a global meat processor that shut major slaughterhouses.
