Medium Regulation — FERC staff report on lessons learned from FY2026 CIP audits

FERC staff CIP audit lessons: late-registered new generation, aggregated low-impact control and AI tools inside CIP networks

On 13 October FERC’s Office of Electric Reliability published a staff report on lessons from its fiscal-2026 CIP audits. Most cybersecurity processes reviewed met the mandatory standards, but auditors found three recurring gaps. Some owners registered new generation late, or had not categorized BES Cyber Systems under CIP-002-5.1a, including inverter-based resources already synchronized and above the threshold; staff traces this to misreading the Commercial Operation Date, and one entity delayed registration for more than a year.
